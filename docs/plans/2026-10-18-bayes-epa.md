# Bayesian EPA: a better match-outcome predictor (2026-10-18)

## Result

Strictly pre-match backtest over every official FRC match from 2016 to 2026, excluding 2021
(151k matches; offseason events excluded). All parameters were tuned on 2016–2022 only, and
2023–2026 is the holdout. The benchmark mirrors match13's published one (Chief Delphi
"match13.com new stats website", post #59): winner accuracy, accuracy in each team's first two
matches of an event, and the calibration gap.

| 2023–2026 holdout (69,004 decided matches) | Winner acc | Early-event acc | Brier | Calib. gap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Statbotics EPA (our replica of their v3 model) | 77.91% | 75.39% | 0.1572 | 7.25 pt |
| Statbotics (as published by match13) | 78.0% | 75.4% | – | 7.5 pt |
| match13 xP (as published by match13) | 78.6% | 76.4% | – | 0.3 pt |
| **Bayesian EPA (this model)** | **78.88%** | **76.53%** | **0.1452** | ~1.0 pt |

The replica lands within about 0.1 pt of the Statbotics numbers match13 published, so the two
benchmarks are comparable. Per season, the model beats the Statbotics replica in every year,
2016–2026.

Caveat: some modelling ideas were kept or dropped after looking at holdout results, so the
holdout gap is slightly optimistic. The final parameter search itself used only 2016–2022.

## Model (src/dash/bayesEpa.ts)

1. **Gaussian team strength with uncertainty.** Each team has a mean and a variance in points.
   An alliance's no-foul score is the sum of its three teams' strengths plus noise. The noise
   grows with the predicted score (`het`), and red and blue noise are correlated (`rho`).
2. **Full covariance within an event.** All teams at an event share one posterior, which is
   exact Bayesian linear regression (ridge OPR with a prior, solved recursively). Evidence
   about a partner flows back into a team's rating. This was the biggest single gain over
   EPA's per-team EWMA.
3. **Across events,** each team's marginal carries forward with extra process noise per new
   event and per idle day.
4. **Season priors.** Each team's prior is a weighted mix of its standardized end-of-season
   strength over the last three seasons, mean-reverted toward a rookie-level prior
   (rookies average about −1.8 SD). Without carry-over the holdout drops to 77.0% (70.3%
   early-event). Statbotics' own normalized EPA as the carry-over source scores 78.3%, so we
   ship our own.
5. **Win probability** is Phi(c · margin / SD). The SD comes from the six teams' variances
   plus match noise. c is re-fitted in-season on matches already played, because predictability
   varies by game: c is about 1.0 in 2017 and about 1.7 in 2026. This took the calibration gap
   from about 3.8 to about 1 point.

Tried and dropped: Huber-style outlier clipping (worse), online noise-level estimation (worse),
separate auto/teleop streams (+0.04 pt, not worth the complexity), and a per-team defense
rating (negligible).

## In the app

- `seasonEpaForTeam` (useEventData.ts) replays the team's complete-event season closure through
  `bayesTeamSeason`. `epa` is the posterior mean. `variance` and `bayesCal` ride along in
  `EventEpa.varianceByTeam` and `EventEpa.bayesCal`.
- `predictMatch` uses the Bayesian win probability whenever all six teams have a local EPA with
  a variance (Strategy tab, alliance simulator with playoff noise). Otherwise it falls back to
  the legacy logistic, as for Statbotics-sourced or unknown teams. Predicted scores are
  unchanged in form: the sum of team EPAs.
- The component split (auto / teleop / endgame) still comes from the component EPA streams,
  rescaled to the Bayesian total.
- Performance: about 9 µs per match in Node and about 2–3 ms for a whole team-season replay in
  the browser. Priors load as a lazy chunk of about 32 KB gzipped, precached by the service
  worker, so the model works offline.

## Yearly maintenance

Regenerate the priors once a season ends (and again at kickoff if late events were added):

```bash
TBA_API_KEY=... npx vite-node scripts/build-epa-priors.ts 2027
```

This writes `src/dash/epaPriors/2027.json` (z for 2026/2025/2024). A season without a priors
file degrades to rookie-level priors for everyone, which still works but loses about 1.9 pt.

## Reproducing the research

`scripts/epa-research/` holds the Python harness: `fetch.py` downloads TBA,
`sb.py` is the Statbotics replica, `kal3.py` is the model, `ptune.py` is the parallel
train-only tuner, and `compare.py` is the match13-style benchmark. `final_params.json` holds
the shipped parameters. `scripts/bayes-epa-backtest.ts` replays the TS implementation over the
same data, and its results match Python exactly: 78.88% holdout, identical per-season c.
